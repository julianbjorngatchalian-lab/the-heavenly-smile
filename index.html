Questions before you book

Do you take my insurance?

We work with most PPO dental plans. Tell us your insurance when you book and we'll check your benefits before your visit.

What happens at the first visit?

An exam with the dentist, a professional cleaning and digital X-rays. You leave with a written care plan and prices. It takes about 60 minutes.

I'm nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us when you book. We explain each step first, agree a signal for breaks, and never rush.

I have tooth pain right now.

Call (512) 555-0142 and we'll do our best to see you the same day. You can also book online and choose "Tooth pain or emergency".
